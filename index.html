1. Number System

संख्या पद्धति

Open lesson | पाठ खोलें

English

Numbers are used for counting, measuring and calculating. Natural numbers: 1, 2, 3, ... Whole numbers: 0, 1, 2, 3, ... Integers include negative numbers, zero and positive numbers.

हिंदी

संख्याओं का उपयोग गिनने, मापने और गणना करने में होता है। प्राकृतिक संख्याएँ: 1, 2, 3, ... पूर्ण संख्याएँ: 0, 1, 2, 3, ... पूर्णांक में ऋणात्मक संख्याएँ, शून्य और धनात्मक संख्याएँ शामिल हैं।

Example | उदाहरण: −3, 0 and 5 are integers. −3, 0 और 5 पूर्णांक हैं।

Practice | अभ्यास: Is zero a whole number? क्या शून्य एक पूर्ण संख्या है?

Show answer | उत्तर देखें

Yes. हाँ।

2. Fractions

भिन्न

Open lesson | पाठ खोलें

English

A fraction represents part of a whole. In 3/4, 3 is the numerator and 4 is the denominator.

हिंदी

भिन्न किसी पूरी वस्तु या मात्रा के एक भाग को दर्शाती है। 3/4 में 3 अंश और 4 हर है।

Example | उदाहरण: 1/4 + 2/4 = 3/4

Practice | अभ्यास: 2/5 + 1/5 = ?

Show answer | उत्तर देखें

3/5

💯

3. Percentage

प्रतिशत

Open lesson | पाठ खोलें

English

Percentage means a number out of 100. Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100.

हिंदी

प्रतिशत का अर्थ है प्रति सौ। प्रतिशत = (भाग ÷ कुल) × 100।

Example | उदाहरण: 25 out of 100 = 25%।

Practice | अभ्यास: What is 20% of 50? 50 का 20% कितना है?

Show answer | उत्तर देखें

10

4. Ratio and Proportion

अनुपात और समानुपात

Open lesson | पाठ खोलें

English

A ratio compares two quantities. A proportion states that two ratios are equal.

हिंदी

अनुपात दो राशियों की तुलना करता है। समानुपात बताता है कि दो अनुपात बराबर हैं।

Example | उदाहरण: 2:4 = 1:2.

Practice | अभ्यास: Simplify 6:9. 6:9 को सरल कीजिए।

Show answer | उत्तर देखें

2:3

5. Profit and Loss

लाभ और हानि

Open lesson | पाठ खोलें

English

Profit = Selling Price − Cost Price, when the selling price is higher. Loss = Cost Price − Selling Price, when the cost price is higher.

हिंदी

जब विक्रय मूल्य, क्रय मूल्य से अधिक होता है, तो लाभ = विक्रय मूल्य − क्रय मूल्य। जब क्रय मूल्य, विक्रय मूल्य से अधिक होता है, तो हानि = क्रय मूल्य − विक्रय मूल्य।

Example | उदाहरण: Cost price ₹100 and selling price ₹120. Profit = ₹20.

Practice | अभ्यास: Cost price ₹200 and selling price ₹180. Find the loss. क्रय मूल्य ₹200 और विक्रय मूल्य ₹180 है। हानि ज्ञात कीजिए।

Show answer | उत्तर देखें

Loss = ₹20 | हानि = ₹20

A few notes about this working website preview.

Why is there no sign-in button?

This version is a front-end website preview. Secure accounts and a real database have not been connected yet, so it does not collect passwords or pretend to create accounts.

Are these official exam questions or syllabuses?

No. The included quiz is sample content to demonstrate how practice works. Official curricula and exam resources should be added only after their source and version are checked.

Does current affairs update automatically?

Not in this version. A reliable current-affairs section needs a maintained, verified source workflow and visible publication dates. No live-news claims are made here.

Do bookmarks stay saved?

Bookmarks are saved in this browser when local storage is available. They are not linked to an account and may not transfer to another device or browser.

What is needed before a public launch?

A secure backend, authentication and role permissions, content review tools, privacy and child-safety checks, accessible interaction testing, and a verified deployment process.
