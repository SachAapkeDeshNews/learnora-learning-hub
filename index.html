A few notes about this working website preview.

Why is there no sign-in button?

This version is a front-end website preview. Secure accounts and a real database have not been connected yet, so it does not collect passwords or pretend to create accounts.

Are these official exam questions or syllabuses?

No. The included quiz is sample content to demonstrate how practice works. Official curricula and exam resources should be added only after their source and version are checked.

Does current affairs update automatically?

Not in this version. A reliable current-affairs section needs a maintained, verified source workflow and visible publication dates. No live-news claims are made here.

Do bookmarks stay saved?

Bookmarks are saved in this browser when local storage is available. They are not linked to an account and may not transfer to another device or browser.

What is needed before a public launch?

A secure backend, authentication and role permissions, content review tools, privacy and child-safety checks, accessible interaction testing, and a verified deployment process.
